Announcement November 2026

Introducing the Mobile Tech Truck: A Classroom on Wheels

We are launching the Mobile Tech Truck, a fully equipped digital classroom built into a bus, to take free AI and digital skills training to underprivileged children and young adults in communities across Nigeria that have no access to tech education. Solar power, computers, internet, air conditioning and video conferencing are already secured. We are now inviting companies, foundations, government agencies and individuals to sponsor the bus and put it on the road.

Training December 2024

IGNITE Program: Empowering 200+ Youth in Abeokuta

Yesdin Technologies partnered with Borderless Tek to deliver an intensive digital skills training program in Abeokuta, Ogun State. Over 200 young Nigerians were equipped with practical skills in WordPress Development, Data Analytics, and Digital Marketing — free of charge. The program, supported by the Nigeria Computer Society, was one of the largest grassroot tech capacity-building initiatives in the region.

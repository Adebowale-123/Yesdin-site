The Problem We Are Solving

Millions of young Nigerians are being left out of the digital economy

Technology is creating new jobs, businesses and opportunities every day, but not everyone can reach them. In rural and underserved communities, children and young adults grow up with no computer, no internet and no teacher who can show them how to use technology to build a future.

Our mission is to teach coding and digital skills to children across Nigeria at no cost to them. A portion of every international enrolment in our paid programmes sponsors a child locally, so access stays inclusive and can keep growing.

Our Solution · Taking Tech to the People

A Digital Classroom on Wheels

The Mobile Tech Truck is a fully equipped, tech-enabled classroom built into a bus. It drives to schools and communities where tech education doesn't exist, and teaches there.

Reach the unreached

Bring structured tech learning to communities where it doesn't exist today.
